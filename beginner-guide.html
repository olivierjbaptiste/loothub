GTA 6 Beginner's Guide: 6 Things to Know First

Guide · Published November 22, 2026

GTA 6's opening hours work a little differently from past games — you can't bank your money straight away, the day/night cycle is much longer, and a lot of the world is only reachable if you know what to look for. These six things will save you cash and time early on. Each is Reported · VGC — drawn from Video Games Chronicle's published beginner's guide, not yet tested by us; we'll mark anything we confirm on our own PS5.

1. Protect your cash before you can bank it

You don't get a bank account until a story mission called "Buck Me," so until then everything you earn is cash on hand — and you lose what you're carrying if you're busted or killed. Two habits matter early: don't hoard a fortune before you can deposit it, and use the stash spot. In Jason's bedroom there are loose floorboards you can use to physically store cash at the house. Drop money off often, especially before anything risky.

2. Treat the day/night cycle as a tool

Days and nights run much longer than in previous GTA games, and the time of day actually changes what you can do. Some shops are closed at night, some missions only trigger during the day or at night, and police behave differently after dark. Open your phone (up on the D-pad) to check the clock, and sleep at Jason's house to skip ahead when you need a different time of day.

3. Use Focus Mode to find hidden rooms

A surprising number of interiors — back rooms, staircases, stashes — aren't obvious from the street. Stand near the back of a shop or building and watch the minimap: it shows whether there's more space inside and whether a door is permanently locked. Use Focus Mode to read doors: a white door can be entered, a red door is alarmed. It's the single best habit for finding the robbery back-entries the money guide talks about.

4. Read the map's building colors

On the world map, buildings are colored to tell you what you can enter. Dark blue buildings have an entrance or interior; light blue ones can't be entered. Near an enterable building the minimap marks the actual door, usually with a short white line. Learn this and you stop wasting time walking into walls.

5. Grab an early Focus Mode boost

Jason has a pet snake named Dolores. Feeding her — watching her eat a mouse — gives you an early buff to Focus Mode. It's a small, easy thing to do in the first hours that pays off every time you use Focus Mode to case a building or line up a shot.

6. Repair your car on the road

After the "Just A Scratch" mission unlocks the service, you can call your mechanic to repair your car wherever you are, in most situations. It costs money, but a quick repair mid-chase or after a wreck is usually worth more than the fee — especially if you're driving something you'd rather not replace.
