About Loothub

Free, fast, independent tools and guides for Grand Theft Auto 6.

Loothub is a fan-made companion site built for players who want to spend less time guessing and more time playing. When a game as big as GTA 6 arrives, the same questions get asked millions of times over — which business makes the most money, where every collectible is hidden, how to reach 100% completion. Loothub exists to answer those questions with clean, interactive tools instead of walls of text.

What you'll find here

Everything on Loothub is designed to be quick to use and genuinely useful:

  • Business ROI Calculator — rank every business by profit per hour, payback time, and return on investment, and toggle upgrades to see what actually pays off. This tool is live now.
  • Interactive Map — every collectible, stunt jump and hidden location, filterable, with progress you can check off. Arriving at launch.
  • 100% Checklist — track missions, collectibles and challenges toward full completion, with progress saved on your device. Arriving at launch.
  • Money Methods — every money-making method ranked by dollars-per-hour, updated as the in-game economy settles. Arriving at launch.

Why it's live early

GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026. Loothub went live months ahead of launch on purpose: so the tools are tested and ready, and so that within hours of release we can fill them with real, verified data as the community maps the game. The goal is for Loothub to be accurate and useful on day one, not weeks later.

Who runs it

Loothub is an independent project built and maintained by a solo developer who builds data-driven tools and dashboards. It isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive in any way — it's simply made by a fan of the series, for fans of the series.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or suggestions for a tool you'd like to see? Get in touch at ojbprojects.dev@gmail.com. Feedback on the data and tools is always welcome — accuracy matters here.
