About Loothub

Free, fast, independent tools and guides for Grand Theft Auto 6.

Loothub is a fan-made companion site built for players who want to spend less time guessing and more time playing. When a game as big as GTA 6 arrives, the same questions get asked millions of times over — which business makes the most money, where every collectible is hidden, how to reach 100% completion. Loothub exists to answer those questions with clean, interactive tools instead of walls of text.

What you'll find here

Everything published today is information you can actually use before launch, drawn from official Rockstar announcements and reputable reporting:

  • Release & editions — the confirmed release date and platforms, and a full editions & pre-order breakdown (prices, bonuses, the collector's box).
  • The game itself — plain-English pages on the characters, the map of Leonida, gameplay & features, vehicles, and the radio & soundtrack.
  • Guides — practical reading on making money, businesses and 100% completion, based on how the series has worked and what's confirmed for GTA 6.

Our approach

We only publish what Rockstar has officially confirmed or what reputable outlets have reported, and we clearly label anything that's analysis, expectation or rumor. We also build interactive tools — a business ROI calculator, a collectibles map and a 100% checklist — but we're holding those back until we can populate them with real, verified GTA 6 data after launch, rather than filling them with invented numbers. GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026, and the goal is for Loothub to be accurate and genuinely useful, not padded.

Who runs it

Loothub is an independent project built and maintained by a solo developer who builds data-driven tools and dashboards. It isn't affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive in any way — it's simply made by a fan of the series, for fans of the series.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or suggestions for a tool you'd like to see? Get in touch at ojbprojects.dev@gmail.com. Feedback on the data and tools is always welcome — accuracy matters here.
