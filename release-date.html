GTA 6 Release Date & Everything We Know

Updated October 2026

After more than a decade of waiting, Grand Theft Auto VI finally has a firm release date. Below is a clear, no-hype rundown of everything Rockstar Games has officially confirmed so far — and where the line sits between fact and speculation.

When does GTA 6 come out?

GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026. That date is the current one listed on Rockstar's official materials, arrived at after two delays — the game was originally targeted for 2025, then moved to May 2026, before settling on the November date for final polish. As of mid-2026, Take-Two has repeatedly reaffirmed the timeline, and the marketing campaign is already underway, which makes another delay unlikely at this stage.

What platforms is it on?

At launch, GTA 6 is coming to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. It's a current-generation title — there are no announced versions for PS4 or Xbox One. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or any PC release date. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both reached PC roughly a year or more after their console launches, which is why many PC players expect GTA 6 to follow eventually — but that's a historical pattern, not a confirmed plan, and no date exists. Treat any specific PC-release year you see online as speculation.

Where is GTA 6 set?

The game returns to Vice City, Rockstar's Miami-inspired metropolis, now dragged into the modern day. Vice City sits within a larger fictional state called Leonida, modeled on Florida. Beyond the city itself, Rockstar has named several regions across Leonida: the Everglades-style wetlands of Grassrivers, the tropical Leonida Keys, the coastal town of Port Gellhorn, Mount Kalaga National Park, and Ambrosia. Rockstar has not published official map dimensions, so treat any specific size figure you see online as unconfirmed.

Who are the protagonists?

For the first time in a mainline entry, GTA 6 features two playable protagonists: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Lucia is the series' first non-optional female lead. The two are a criminal couple whose story Rockstar has likened to Bonnie and Clyde — Jason is a former soldier who fell in with drug runners in the Leonida Keys, while Lucia grew up in Liberty City and served time in Leonida Penitentiary. Players can switch between them, and at certain points control both together during open-world scenarios.

Pre-orders and price

Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, both digitally and physically. Pricing is now official: the Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. The Ultimate Edition bundles the Vintage Vice City Pack plus extra in-game vehicles, apparel, and content. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+, and every pre-order gets the Vintage Vice City Pack bonus. There is no collector's edition — the closest thing is a separate ~$400 "Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" merch set that does not include the game. Note that physical copies ship as a download code in the box, with no disc.

For the full breakdown of both editions, every bonus, and where to pre-order, see our GTA 6 pre-order & editions guide.

Will there be a GTA Online?

We're not going to overstate this: Rockstar has not announced the details of a GTA 6 online mode. There's no official confirmation of when (or exactly how) an online mode launches relative to the single-player game, how it's structured, or whether any progress carries over from GTA V's online mode. You'll see confident claims online about timing and a "fresh start" — treat those as expectation and rumor, not fact. We'll update this section if and when Rockstar publishes official details.
