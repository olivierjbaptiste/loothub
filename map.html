The GTA 6 Map: The State of Leonida

GTA 6 returns to Vice City — but this time it's one city inside a whole state. Here's what Rockstar has actually shown of Leonida, and what's still unknown until the map goes live at launch.

Updated October 2026 · Based on official trailers and Rockstar materials

What's confirmed: Leonida and Vice City

GTA 6 is set in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on modern-day Florida, with Vice City — the series' long-running Miami analog — as its centerpiece. This was established with the first official trailer and reinforced in Rockstar's follow-up marketing. Unlike Vice City's earlier appearances, the city is now set within a far larger surrounding state rather than being the whole map.

The regions Rockstar has named

Rockstar has officially named several regions across Leonida (see Rockstar's official GTA VI screenshots):

  • Vice City — the dense, neon-lit coastal metropolis modeled on Miami; the urban heart of the map.
  • Leonida Keys — a tropical island archipelago evoking the Florida Keys.
  • Grassrivers — the Everglades-style wetlands.
  • Port Gellhorn — a coastal town on Leonida's quieter coast.
  • Ambrosia — an inland area associated with a sugar refinery and a local biker gang.
  • Mount Kalaga National Park — a wilderness area along the state's northern border.

What's not confirmed is the full list of neighborhoods within each region and how they connect. We're not publishing invented district names or a fabricated street map to fill that gap — plenty of sites do, and it isn't reliable.

How big is the map?

Rockstar has not published official map dimensions. Expect the usual pre-launch estimates and leaks to circulate; we'll only add figures here once there's an official source or a verified in-game measurement after release.
