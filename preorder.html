Pre-order bonuses

  • Vintage Vice City Pack — the main pre-order bonus, included with every edition when you pre-order.
  • Free month of GTA+ — bundled with digital pre-orders.
  • Ultimate Edition extras — additional vehicles, apparel, and in-game content on top of the pack.

Digital vs. physical

Per Rockstar's official editions page and support documentation:

  • Standard Edition — "Digital or Code in Box." The physical version contains a download code inside the box; Rockstar confirms no disc is included.
  • Ultimate Edition — digital only. Rockstar states plainly that "a physical version of the Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition is not available." There is no boxed Ultimate Edition.

If you buy the physical (Code in Box) Standard Edition and later want the Ultimate content, Rockstar lets you purchase the Ultimate Edition Upgrade — a digital add-on — from the PlayStation or Microsoft stores after redeeming your Standard code. Always confirm the current edition, format and price on the official store for your region before buying.

Heads up on the free GTA+ month. The complimentary month of GTA+ offered with digital pre-orders is a subscription promotion. Promotions like this typically renew automatically at the standard price after the free month unless you cancel beforehand, subject to the terms shown at sign-up. Check and manage it in your platform's subscription settings, and review the current GTA+ terms on Rockstar's site before the month ends.

Is there a collector's edition?

Not a game edition, no. The closest thing is the separate "The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" — a roughly $399 merchandise box set that does not include the game. If you want to play GTA 6, you still need the Standard or Ultimate Edition on top of it. (Confirmed by Rockstar; see coverage at Push Square and TechRadar.)

What about PC?

There is no PC version at launch — GTA 6 is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only on November 19, 2026. Rockstar has not announced a PC version at all, so any specific PC date is speculation. It's fair to note that GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both came to PC roughly a year or more after their console launches, which is why many expect an eventual PC release — but that's a pattern, not a confirmed plan, and no timeframe has been announced.
