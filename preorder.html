Digital vs. physical

Both editions come in digital and physical versions. One thing to know about the physical copies: they ship as a download code in the box — there is no disc. If you want something fully on-disc, that option doesn't exist for GTA 6. Digital pre-orders have the edge on bonuses, since the free month of GTA+ is tied to digital.

Is there a collector's edition?

No. Rockstar has not announced a physical collector's edition, and has given no indication one is coming later. The closest thing is the separate "Goodtime State – Vice City Collection" — a roughly $400 merchandise set that does not include the game. If you want to play GTA 6, you still need the Standard or Ultimate Edition on top of it.

What about PC?

There is no PC version at launch — GTA 6 is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only on November 19, 2026. Based on Rockstar's history with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, a PC release is widely expected roughly one to two years later (2027–2028), but nothing has been officially confirmed.
