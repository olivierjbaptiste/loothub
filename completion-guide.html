GTA 6 100% Completion & Collectibles Guide

Guide · Updated for launch

Reaching 100% completion has been a tradition in Grand Theft Auto since the very first 3D games, and it's one of the most rewarding ways to experience everything a map has to offer. For a world as large as GTA 6's Leonida, hitting full completion will be a serious undertaking — which is exactly why having a plan, and a way to track your progress, matters. Here's what to expect and how to approach it.

What usually counts toward 100%

Rockstar hasn't published the official GTA 6 completion checklist yet, but across the series, full completion has typically required a mix of the following:

  • Story missions — completing the main campaign from start to finish.
  • Collectibles — finding every hidden item scattered across the map (more on these below).
  • Side activities and minigames — things like races, sports, and other optional pastimes, often with target scores or wins.
  • Random encounters and strangers — optional characters and events out in the open world that each need to be completed.
  • Challenges — skill-based or activity-based goals, such as distances traveled, stunts performed, or specific feats.

The exact list and percentages will be confirmed once the game is out, but if you've chased 100% in a past GTA, this structure will feel familiar.

Collectibles to expect

Collectibles are the part of completion that sends most players to a map, because they're deliberately hidden and easy to miss. GTA games have historically featured collectible sets such as hidden packages, spaceship parts, letter scraps, stunt jumps, and similar map-wide hunts. GTA 6 is expected to continue this tradition with its own sets unique to Leonida. The smart approach is to knock out collectibles in batches by region rather than chasing them randomly across the whole map — it saves an enormous amount of travel time.

Track it instead of memorizing it

The single biggest time-saver for completion is a good tracker. Trying to remember which of dozens or hundreds of collectibles you've already grabbed is how people end up re-checking the same spots and missing others entirely.
