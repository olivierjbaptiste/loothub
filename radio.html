GTA 6 Radio Stations & Soundtrack

Music has always been half the soul of a GTA game, and Vice City's airwaves are shaping up to be the series' deepest yet. Here's every radio station Rockstar has officially revealed, plus the new podcast and on-foot features.

Updated October 2026 · Based on Rockstar's official reveal

Rockstar has revealed six stations so far, with more expected before launch.

Rockstar has said the full lineup spans everything from classic rock to talk radio, so these six are only the first wave — expect several more stations and hundreds of licensed tracks to be revealed closer to launch.

On-demand podcasts — a series first

GTA 6 introduces on-demand podcasts tailored to Jason and Lucia — the first time the series has done podcasts. Unlike normal radio, these can be played on demand rather than waiting for them to come up in rotation, adding another layer to the characters and the world of Leonida.

Listen anywhere — on foot, too

You're no longer tied to a car radio. Players can listen through headphones and earbuds connected to their in-game smartphone, so your station (or podcast) keeps playing while you're walking around on foot. It's a small change that makes the world feel a lot more modern.

The soundtrack

Beyond the stations, Rockstar has announced an official soundtrack album of original music, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (reported as 34 tracks), with artists including Travis Scott, PinkPantheress and Yung Lean and several singles already released. You can preview the six stations on Rockstar's official music site at rockstargames.com/VI/music. The six revealed stations are only the first wave — Rockstar has said the complete radio soundtrack hasn't been revealed yet.

Station lineup and album details confirmed by Rockstar, as compiled by RouteNote. The on-air hosts are played by named artists — including Bad Bunny, Burna Boy, Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya, Robyn, Trick Daddy and Trina, and Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey.

We'll keep this updated as Rockstar reveals more stations, hosts, and the full track list in the run-up to November 19, 2026.
