GTA 6 Vehicles: Cars, Bikes, Boats & Aircraft

From supercars tearing down Vice Beach to airboats in the Grassrivers, GTA 6's garage spans land, air and sea. Here's what's been shown so far, grouped by type.

Updated October 2026 · Identified from official trailers and the August gameplay reveal

Before the list: Rockstar hasn't published an official vehicle list. The rides below are the ones players have identified in Rockstar's trailers, screenshots and the August "Extended Look." Expect many more at launch — GTA games ship with hundreds of vehicles, and Rockstar always holds plenty back.

Supercars & sports cars

The exotics are front and center in Vice City. GTA uses its own fictional brands (Grotti, Pegassi, Truffade and the like) that riff on real manufacturers. Spotted so far include the Grotti Furia, Pegassi Tempesta, Truffade Thrax and Pegassi Zorrusso on the supercar end, plus sports cars like the Obey 8F Drafter, Bravado Banshee, Grotti Carbonizzare and Enus Paragon R.

Muscle & classic cars

Leonida's retro streak runs deep. Expect muscle like the Vapid Dominator, Bravado Gauntlet, Declasse Sabre Turbo and Imponte Ruiner, alongside classics such as the Grotti Cheetah '95, Vapid Stanier '55 and the Ubermacht Sentinel Classic — the kind of cars that fit the neon-and-chrome Vice City vibe.

SUVs, trucks & everyday vehicles

The world is filled out with SUVs, pickups, vans and work trucks, plus the usual police and emergency fleet (cruisers, sheriff SUVs and armored riot trucks). These are the backbone of traffic and a big part of what makes the open world feel lived-in.

Motorcycles & bikes

Two wheels are well represented — sport bikes like the Pegassi Bati, off-road Dinka Enduro dirt bikes, cruisers and choppers from the Western Motorcycle Company, and smaller stuff like scooters, mopeds and BMX bikes for getting around town.

Boats & watercraft

With the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers and open coastline, water travel matters more than ever. Expect speedboats and luxury yachts, jet skis (Seashark-style), airboats/fanboats for the wetlands, kayaks, and large set-pieces like ferries and cruise ships.

Aircraft

The skies include helicopters — attack choppers, news and police helis — plus light planes, crop dusters, private jets and the series-staple blimp. As with every GTA, aircraft tend to unlock as you progress rather than being available from the start.
