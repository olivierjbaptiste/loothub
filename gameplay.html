GTA 6 Gameplay & Features: Everything Confirmed

Rockstar has now shown real gameplay and detailed how Grand Theft Auto VI plays. Here's a clear rundown of everything confirmed so far — from the dual-protagonist structure to the side activities and the living world around them.

Updated October 2026 · Based on official reveals and previews

The August 2026 gameplay reveal

On August 27, 2026, Rockstar released an extended look at GTA 6 — just over 20 minutes of gameplay. It debuted on Netflix before going out to YouTube, and it was the first proper look at the game in motion rather than cinematic trailers. It showcased Vice City and Leonida, the two protagonists in action, driving and combat, and a slice of the open world's density and detail.

Two protagonists, one story

You play as Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a criminal couple Rockstar compares to Bonnie and Clyde. You can switch between them freely, play long stretches as either one solo, or control both together during shared scenarios — including driver-and-passenger setups where one drives while the other handles the action. For the full character breakdown, see our GTA 6 characters page.

Missions & crime

Expect the series' signature mix, built bigger: heists, high-speed car chases, and protection jobs, alongside the open-world chaos GTA is known for. The reveal leaned into the couple's escalating crime spree across Leonida as the backbone of the campaign.

A living, reactive world

Rockstar is leaning hard into immersion. NPCs react to what you do rather than following fixed scripts, and the characters themselves change over time — diet, exercise, and sleep affect their appearance. Layer on an expansive weather system and a world reported to be considerably larger than GTA V's, and the open world is designed to feel alive whether or not you're on a mission.

Radio & music

The radio returns and expands: Rockstar has revealed radio stations that include in-game podcasts — a first for the series — alongside the usual licensed music. A physical soundtrack album has also been confirmed, a sign of how central the Vice City–era music identity is to this entry.

Still to come: Rockstar typically holds back a lot until close to launch. We'll fold in new gameplay details as they're officially shown ahead of November 19, 2026.
