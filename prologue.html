GTA 6 Prologue Walkthrough: Lucia's Opening

Walkthrough · Published November 22, 2026

Spoiler warning. This page describes the opening mission of GTA 6's story, including how it ends. If you'd rather play it cold, bookmark it and come back.

The Prologue is the first thing you play in GTA 6, and it sets up Lucia's whole story: a small robbery that goes wrong, a short chase, and the arrest that lands her in prison. It's also a gentle tutorial — it teaches aiming, threatening a target, quick-time prompts, and reading the minimap during a police search. Here's how it plays out, step by step.

Step by step

  1. In the cell. You begin at Lucia's cell window in Hamlet Prison. Turn around, look at your cellmate and talk to him to start things moving.
  2. The escort. A guard, CO Wilson, opens the cell and walks Lucia toward the counselor's office. He pauses in the common area to take a phone call, then carries on through the corridor. Just follow along.
  3. The flashback begins. The scene with the counselor triggers a cutscene showing the crime that put Lucia here — and then hands you control to play through it.
  4. The robbery. You take over inside a payday-loan / check-cashing store. Aim at the teller, use the threaten prompt, and grab the cash he hands over. As you turn to leave, a bystander grabs Lucia — break free with the quick-time prompt.
  5. The chase. You're out and running from the police. A flashing red-and-blue circle on the bottom-left minimap marks the area they're searching. You're not meant to escape clean here — the scene cuts back to the counselor's office, and you're playing present-day Lucia again.
  6. Back to the coffee shop. With the heat off, you head to a coffee shop marked on the minimap and compass. As you get close, Lucia is arrested — this can't be avoided, and it's the point of the scene.
  7. The ending. A cutscene reveals why she went back: she'd left her baby, Tommy, at the coffee shop. Staff had already reported the child, and he's taken into child welfare. Lucia stays in custody, and her goal becomes clear — get out of prison and get back to her son. The game then moves into the next mission, "Gone Fishing."

What the Prologue teaches you

  • Aiming and threatening — pointing a weapon and using the threaten prompt to make someone comply, rather than shooting.
  • Quick-time prompts — a button press at the right moment to break free of a grab.
  • Reading the police search — using the minimap search circle to understand where police are looking during a chase.

The Prologue is scripted, so there's no "best" strategy to optimize — just follow the beats. The value is in learning the controls before the open world opens up.
