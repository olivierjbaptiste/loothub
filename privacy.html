Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Loothub ("we", "us", or "the site"), available at loothubgg.com, handles information when you visit. We aim to collect as little as possible. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

What the site currently offers

At present, Loothub is an informational site: it provides written guides and reference pages about Grand Theft Auto VI. Our interactive tools — a business ROI calculator, a collectibles map and a 100% completion checklist — are still in development and are not currently public. The sections below describe how data is handled today, and how it will be handled if and when those tools go live.

Information collected when you visit

Loothub does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly ask you for personal information such as your name or email to read the site. However, the site is not entirely anonymous: like most websites, it loads third-party services that process technical information about your visit.

  • Advertising (Google AdSense). We load Google's advertising scripts, which may collect and process technical identifiers — including your IP address, device and browser information, and cookie or similar identifiers — to serve and measure ads. This processing is carried out by Google as a third party under its own policies. See "Cookies and advertising" below.
  • Analytics (Cloudflare). We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general, aggregated traffic patterns. Cloudflare may process technical information such as IP address and request data to provide this service and to protect the site.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information (such as browser type, device type, and general region) for security and performance.

We (Loothub) do not sell your information and do not use it to personally identify you. Some of the third-party providers above may, however, associate technical identifiers with you for advertising or security purposes under their own terms — so your visit is not fully anonymous. Links to the relevant provider policies and your opt-out options are below.

If and when our interactive tools are made public, they are designed to save your preferences and progress (for example, items you check off) locally in your own browser using local storage — that data would stay on your device and would not be transmitted to us.

Cookies and advertising

Loothub may display advertising provided by third-party services, including Google AdSense. To support the site, these services use cookies and similar technologies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site or its ads function.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general traffic patterns (such as which pages are most visited). The reports we see are aggregated and we do not use them to personally identify you; however, Cloudflare processes technical request data (which can include your IP address) to generate them, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Loothub is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date above.
